Who we are at ClubSams Grocery

"We didn't start ClubSams to be the biggest store — we started it to be the smartest place to buy in bulk."

ClubSams Grocery was born from a simple belief: families and small businesses deserve the same buying power as large retailers. For over four years, we've built our reputation one member at a time — not with flashy marketing, but with prices that speak for themselves.

Every week we negotiate directly with manufacturers and suppliers to cut out unnecessary middlemen. That is how we keep our shelves full of food, beverages, home essentials, electronics, clothing and more — all in large formats at prices our competitors struggle to match.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about membership, pricing and shopping at ClubSams Grocery.

Do I need a membership to shop?

Yes. ClubSams Grocery is a membership warehouse club. An active membership is required to purchase at member prices in our clubs and online. Guest shopping is not available for member-priced items.

How much does membership cost?

Club Membership is $45/year, Plus is $100/year and Business is $150/year. Fees are billed annually at enrollment. Applicable sales tax may apply. See our membership section for plan details.

Are the prices on this website guaranteed?

Prices shown are examples of current member pricing and may change without notice. Final price is confirmed at checkout or at the register. Availability varies by location and season.

Do you ship to all 50 states?

Yes — schedule curbside pickup at any ClubSams location or home delivery to most U.S. addresses. Pick hourly pickup windows (9 AM–8 PM) or 2-hour delivery slots. Availability depends on your ZIP code and membership plan. Plus members may qualify for free delivery on eligible orders.

Can businesses buy in bulk?

Yes. Our Business Membership is designed for restaurants, retailers and resellers. Volume pricing, business invoicing and commercial credit may be available for qualified accounts.

How do I contact customer support?

Call 1-800-CLUB-SAMS, email support@clubsamsgrocery.com or use our contact form. Hours: Mon–Fri 8am–8pm ET, Sat–Sun 9am–6pm ET.
